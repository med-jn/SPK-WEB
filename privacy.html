Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

In short: SPK Booster does not collect, store on a server, share, sell or send any personal data. It has no ads, no accounts, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not request the Android internet permission.

This policy is available in English and Arabic. If the two differ in meaning, the English version prevails.

This Privacy Policy applies to the SPK Booster Android app (package name com.spk.bstr, “the App”) and to this website, spk.orcaup.com (“the Website”). Both are provided by ORCAUP (“we”, “us”). You can reach us at contact@orcaup.com.

1. Information we collect through the App

None. The App does not collect, transmit or share any of the following:

Type of dataCollected?
Name, email address or other personal informationNo
LocationNo
Contacts, messages, photos, filesNo
Audio or microphone recordingsNo
Device or advertising identifiersNo
Usage analytics or crash reports sent by the AppNo

The App has no user accounts and does not connect to any server. It does not request the INTERNET permission, so it has no way to send data off your device.

2. Information stored on your device

Your preferences (loudness level, equalizer curve, selected scene and feature switches) are saved locally in the App’s private storage so they are there the next time you open it. This information never leaves your device. The App opts out of Android cloud backup, so these settings are not copied to cloud backups either. They are deleted when you clear the App’s data or uninstall it.

3. Permissions and why the App uses them

  • Modify audio settings (MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS): to apply the equalizer, compressor and limiter to your phone’s audio output.
  • Foreground service – media playback (FOREGROUND_SERVICE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK): keeps the sound engine running while you use other apps. An ongoing notification with a Stop button is shown, and the service stops when you turn the engine off.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): to show that notification on Android 13 and later.
  • Audio recording (RECORD_AUDIO) – optional: used only if you turn on “Live signal animation”. Android groups this ability under the microphone permission. The App uses it solely to read a loudness level (a peak and average value) of the sound currently playing on your phone so the glow animation can follow it. The App does not record, save, analyze the content of, or transmit any audio or microphone input. The feature is off by default, the permission is not requested unless you turn the feature on, and you can revoke it at any time in Android settings.

4. Third parties

The App does not include any third-party advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs, and we do not share data with anyone because we do not collect any.

Installing the App through Google Play is subject to Google’s own terms and privacy policy. If you have chosen to share usage and diagnostics data with Google on your Android device, Google may provide us with aggregated crash or performance statistics through Google Play Console. These do not identify you.

5. This Website

  • The Website does not use cookies, analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and it loads no third-party scripts, fonts or embeds.
  • The Website is hosted by Vercel Inc. Like any web host, it may process standard technical logs (such as IP address, browser type and request time) to deliver pages and keep the service secure. We do not use these logs to identify visitors.
  • If you email us, we receive your email address and the content of your message. We use them only to reply and to handle your request, we keep them only as long as needed for that purpose, and we do not share them.

6. Children

The App is a general-audience utility and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

7. Data retention and deletion

We hold no personal data about App users on any server, so there is nothing for us to delete. To remove everything the App stores locally, use Reset everything in the App’s Settings, clear the App’s storage in Android settings, or uninstall the App. If you have emailed us and want your messages deleted, write to contact@orcaup.com.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal data. Because the App does not collect personal data, there is nothing to access or correct. For emails you have sent us, you can exercise these rights by contacting contact@orcaup.com.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will publish the new version at this address and update the date above. If a future version of the App changes how data is handled, this policy will be updated before that version is released.
