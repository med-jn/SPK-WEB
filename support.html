Frequently asked questions

Why is it not much louder on my phone speaker?

A phone speaker has physical limits, and the App cannot exceed what the hardware can produce. First set your phone’s media volume to high, then use the loudness ring for the rest. Turn on Speaker guard so deep bass the speaker cannot play does not cause distortion, and try a scene such as Clear talk or Video.

The sound crackles or distorts. What should I do?

Lower the loudness level and your phone volume. Make sure Smart limiter and Speaker guard are on in Settings, and keep Extreme mode off.

Why is there a notification while the App runs?

Android requires an ongoing notification for apps that keep an audio engine running in the background. It disappears as soon as you tap Stop in the notification or turn the power button off.

It stops working after a while in the background.

Some phones close background apps aggressively. Open Settings → Tools → Battery settings in the App and allow SPK Booster to run in the background.

Does it work with every app and every device?

Most of the time, yes, but some devices or apps restrict system-wide audio effects. Open Settings and look at the Diagnostics section to see which parts of the audio engine are ready on your phone.

Why does the App mention audio recording permission?

Only for the optional Live signal animation. It reads how loud the playing sound is so the glow can follow it. It is off by default, the permission is not requested unless you turn that feature on, and nothing is recorded, stored or sent.

Does the App collect my data?

No. There are no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and the App has no internet permission. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I remove all my data?

Open Settings → Tools → Reset everything in the App, or clear the App’s storage in Android settings, or uninstall it. All settings are stored only on your device.
