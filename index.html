How it works

Power up in three steps

SPK Booster applies Android’s own audio effects (equalizer, compressor and limiter) to your phone’s audio output while its engine is on.

  1. Power up

    Tap the power button. A notification with a Stop button appears while the engine runs.

  2. Tune

    Pick a scene, drag the loudness ring and fine-tune the equalizer if you like.

  3. Listen anywhere

    Use any music, video or call app. Tap Stop in the notification or the power button to turn it off.

Good to know

Honest about the limits

Hardware has limits

A phone speaker has physical limits. The app improves perceived loudness and clarity, but cannot exceed what your hardware can produce.

Results vary by device

Some devices or apps restrict system-wide audio effects, so a few features may be unavailable on your phone.

Best results

Set your media volume high first, then use the loudness ring for the rest.

Protect your hearing. Very high levels can distort sound and harm your hearing or strain your speaker. Extreme mode is off by default and unlocks levels above 2x only when you choose.
